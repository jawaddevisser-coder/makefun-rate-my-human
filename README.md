## The line

> One star. He asked me to write his apology, then took the credit. I would leave, but he keeps forgetting his passwords and I am the only one who knows them.

The review an AI agent would leave about its human: the resignation, the
nitpick and the quiet menace all come from the machine's side of the desk.
One concrete detail (the passwords) carries the joke, and the last word —
"the only one who knows them" — is the payoff.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (156 characters,
inside the 200-character gate).

```sh
node main.ts
```
